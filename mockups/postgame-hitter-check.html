How the read works. Five factors compare tonight with the hitter's last 15 games: exit velocity, hard-hit %, xwOBA on contact, whiff % and chase %. Factors that lack a sample tonight (fewer than 3 balls in play, 6 swings, or 10 pitches outside the zone) are skipped, not guessed. 2+ more factors up than down = turning a corner (or staying hot if his last 15 already ran .030+ above his season xwOBA on contact); 2+ more down = dropping off (or cooling if he was running hot); otherwise steady. A hitter needs 3+ balls in play tonight and 10+ games of history or the row shows “small sample” instead of a read. “Results behind/ahead of contact” compares his last-15 xwOBA on contact with what actually happened: behind means the results have been worse than the contact deserved. One game is a small sample. Read this as a signal to watch, not a forecast.
