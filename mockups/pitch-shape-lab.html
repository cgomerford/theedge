§ 01The question

§ 02How they win

Movement map

Each square is a 2-inch movement cell, coloured by whiffs per swing across the league. Dots are his pitches; the ring covers ±1 SD.

Whiff% = whiffs ÷ swings. Cells under 150 swings (all velocities, all arm slots) are hatched, not coloured. League sample 2024–2026, right-handed pitchers of this type.

§ 05Method

Neighborhood lookup. We take the bin his average shape falls in, plus one bin either side in velocity (1 mph), IVB (2 in) and HB (2 in) — at most 27 bins, same pitch type, hand and arm-slot band (10°). Bins closer to his shape count a little more. Rates come from summed counts, never from averaging rates.

150 swingsbefore we show whiff%
300 pitchesbefore we show CSW%
100 batted ballsbefore we show xwOBA on contact

If a neighborhood is too thin we widen to ±2 bins and say so. If it is still too thin we show an empty state — no number.
