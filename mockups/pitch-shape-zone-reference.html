Pitch Shape Lab · The Pitch Map

Putting the strike zone next to the movement map

The map plots how much a pitch moves (inches), not where it crosses the plate (feet), so a zone drawn on it would sit at an arbitrary spot. Two ways to give the zone as a reference.

Synthetic. In the app: a precomputed plate-location table per movement cell (pitches counted by 0.1 ft cell, same pipeline as pitch_shape_cells); a spot with too few pitches shows no panel instead of a guess. Batter’s view, inside on the left, whichever side he bats.

A · Scale inset

Good: no new data, ships in a day, never misleads if labelled “scale only”.

Limit: it answers “how big is the zone next to this much break”, not “where do these pitches end up”.

B · Location panel

Good: answers “where do pitches with this shape live relative to the zone”, which the movement map alone cannot.

Cost: one new precomputed table and a weekly build, plus a thin-sample rule for sparse spots.
