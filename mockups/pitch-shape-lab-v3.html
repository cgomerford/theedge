Sources, method and minimum samples

  • Pitches: Baseball Savant tracking data for MLB, read from the lab's own tables. Right-handed four-seam fastballs only in this mockup.
  • Shape: ride is induced vertical break and run is horizontal break, both in inches, as measured at the plate; left-handers are flipped so arm side is always to the right.
  • League results by shape: every league four-seam of that hand, 2024-26 pooled, in 0.1 inch cells (grouped here into 0.25 inch squares), summed inside a window of 0.5, 1 or 2 inches each way.
  • Minimums: a whiff rate needs 40+ swings; runs saved per 100 needs 300+ pitches; wOBA and xwOBA need 100+ plate appearances ending in a result; batting average and slugging need 100+ at-bats. Under those, the page shows an empty state, never an estimate.
  • Sample label: STRONG is 200+ swings in the window, MODERATE is 100 to 199, THIN is under 100.
  • Months: a month needs 50+ pitches to show speed, ride and run, 40+ swings for whiffs per swing and 25+ balls in play for xwOBA on contact. Months are available for MLB 2025 and 2026 only; no interpolation across a missing month.
  • What moved the shape: two full months (50+ pitches each) at least two months apart in one season, with speed moved 1 mph or ride or run moved 1 inch. This describes what changed and says nothing about why.
  • Leaderboard: pitchers with 100+ pitches of the type. Shape Score pools all seasons for the shape side; his own results use 2026. The comparison with his own results is judged from 300 pitches, with a band of 0.4 runs per 100.
  • Levels: Triple-A and Florida State League, 2026, shape only.
