Why it is built this way

    
    
      
  • 16:9, 1200×675. Shown uncropped in the feed; the number tiles stay readable at phone width (the small preview above is that size).
    • 
      
  • Three reads, not twelve. One hook per row: a number you can quote, a plain-language line, and a tiny chart. Big type, hard black rules, yellow block for the matchup — it should stop a scroll.
    • 
      
  • Which reads appear is picked from the data. The card shows the three strongest rows that clear their sample gates (e.g. a zone-clash row only if 3+ hitters have a real edge; a late-innings row only with 3+ meetings). Nothing appears when the sample is too thin.
    • 
      
  • House rules. No Edge Score, no percentages of winning, no picks or odds language. Wording describes what the numbers show. The link lives in the reply, never the post body.
    • 
      
  • Text is ~190 characters (limit 250), leaving room for the team hashtags or a hook line if you want one.
