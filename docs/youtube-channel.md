## Description (paste into About, 791 characters; the first 150 show in search)

Baseball analytics for the fan. x9Lab turns public MLB pitch and batted-ball data into something you can see: game previews, pitch breakdowns, and what changed for a pitcher or a hitter, with the sample behind every number.

I'm George, a baseball fan from the UK with a data science background. It started at a Phillies game, when the scoreboard showed numbers I had never seen at a ballpark and I wanted to understand all of them. These videos are the tools I wished I had.

We describe what has happened and how often. We don't give betting tips or forecasts.

Everything here is built on public game data. The interactive version, with the maps and labs, is at https://xlab9.com
